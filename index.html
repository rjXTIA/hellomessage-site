📞  Calls and video calls

Open any chat and you will see two buttons at the top right — a phone for a voice call and a camera for a video call. The person you are calling gets a ringing screen with Accept and Decline. Once they accept, you are connected, with mute, camera on-off and end-call controls. Calls go directly between the two phones using WebRTC, and Supabase only carries the introduction that lets the two phones find each other.
