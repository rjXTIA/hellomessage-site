In plain words: Hell'o Message lets you chat with people you choose. To do that we store your email, the name you pick, and the messages you send. We do not sell your data, we do not read your messages, and we never store your password in a readable form.

Hell'o Message ("the app", "we", "us") is a real-time messaging application. By creating an account or using the app you agree to these Terms and to the Privacy Policy set out below. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. If you are under the age of majority in your country (18 in India), you may use the app only with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian. The app is not directed at children below 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

3. Your account

  • You sign in with an email address and a password, or with a supported third-party sign-in such as Google.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password and device secure, and for activity that happens under your account.
  • You agree to provide accurate information and not to impersonate anyone else.
  • You may stop using the app and request deletion of your account at any time.

4. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and the display name you choose.
  • Authentication data: your password is handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a salted cryptographic hash. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Messages and content: the messages and content you send through the app, together with the time they were sent and the conversation they belong to.
  • Presence data: whether you are currently online and the last time you were active, so other users can see your availability.
  • Basic technical data: information needed to keep the service working, such as connection and error information.

We do not collect your contacts, your precise location, your photos, your microphone recordings, or your camera, unless you deliberately choose to share such content inside a chat — in which case it forms part of the message you chose to send.

5. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To deliver your messages to the people you are chatting with, in real time.
  • To show presence, typing and read status.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent abuse, and fix faults.
  • To show aggregate usage numbers (for example, how many accounts exist) to the service operator — as counts only, never with your identity or content attached.

6. What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not read, scan or mine the content of your private messages.
  • We do not store your password in readable form, and no one — including the operator — can view it.
  • We do not show your email address or message content in operator analytics.

7. Where your data lives and how it is protected

Your data is stored on our hosting provider's managed database infrastructure (Supabase, hosted in the Mumbai region). We protect it with:

  • Row Level Security at the database level, so that an account can only ever read conversations it is a member of.
  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all communication between the app and the service.
  • Hashed passwords, so that even in the event of a database breach your password cannot be recovered from it.

8. Sharing your data

We share data only with the people you are chatting with, and with the service providers we need to run the app (such as our hosting and authentication provider, acting on our instructions). We may disclose data if the law requires it, but we will limit any such disclosure to what is legally required.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data and messages for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile and the conversations tied to it are removed from our systems, subject to any short period needed for backups and legal obligations.
